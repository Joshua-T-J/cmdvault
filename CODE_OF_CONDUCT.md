# Code of Conduct

GistVault is a small, friendly open-source project. We want everyone who takes part — in issues,
pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project meets — to feel welcome and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before jumping to conclusions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time, privacy and differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing conversations.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this
code, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Reports will be handled promptly and kept confidential as far as possible.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.
